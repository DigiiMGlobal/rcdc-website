01 About RCDC

Rotaract Club of Dahisar Coast

The youth wing of the Rotary Club of Mumbai Dahisar, and a home for young changemakers.

Rotaract Club of Dahisar Coast is a youth-driven community built on friendship, leadership, service and impact. We bring together young people who believe that real change starts when people come together and give their ideas a purpose.

Through community initiatives, professional development, fellowship, international connections and real chances to lead, RCDC gives young people room to grow and leave their mark, instead of watching from the sidelines.

Over the years, RCDC has become much more than a club. It is a community built on friendships, shared responsibilities and a common wish to make a difference.

01 · Who we are

The youth wing of the Rotary Club of Mumbai Dahisar, part of Rotaract District 3141, Zone 2. Anyone 18 or older can join.

02 · What we believe

Change begins with taking initiative. When young people come together with intent, even the smallest idea can create a ripple.

03 · What we do

Nine avenues of work, from community service and professional growth to fellowship, international service and creative campaigns.

04 · Why we exist

To create a generation that doesn't just witness change, but leads it.

    02 Theme · RIY 2026–27

    Cosmic Renaissance.

    A new universe. A new chapter. Limitless possibilities. Every year marks a new chapter, but some chapters are meant to redefine the universe.

    Cosmic Renaissance marks the beginning of a new era for RCDC: a year of rebirth, exploration, transformation and limitless potential.

    Inspired by the vastness of the cosmos, this year's vision sees every member as a celestial force with their own light, orbit and purpose. Together, we form a constellation where individual brilliance becomes collective impact.

    Just as galaxies are born from countless stars coming together, Cosmic Renaissance is about bringing people, ideas and possibilities together to create something greater than ourselves.
